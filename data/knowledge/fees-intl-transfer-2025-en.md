title: International transfer fees (2025 tariff)
lang: en
topic: fees
valid_from: 2025-01-01
valid_to: 2025-12-31
---
SEPA transfers in euros are free. Transfers outside the SEPA area cost 0.2% of the amount, with a minimum of 20 EUR and a maximum of 80 EUR, plus correspondent bank fees. Instant transfers cost 1 EUR. This tariff applies from 1 January to 31 December 2025.
