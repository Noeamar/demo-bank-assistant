title: Using your card abroad
lang: en
topic: cards
valid_from: 2025-01-01
---
Card payments in euros within the euro area are free. Payments in other currencies cost 2% of the amount. Cash withdrawals in other currencies cost 2% plus 3 EUR per withdrawal. Before travelling, check your payment limits in the app; you can raise them temporarily for 30 days.
