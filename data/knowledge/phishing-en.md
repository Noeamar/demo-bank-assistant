title: Phishing: Demo Bank will never ask for your codes
lang: en
topic: security
valid_from: 2025-01-01
---
Demo Bank will never ask you for your passwords, secure codes or full card number by phone, SMS or email, and will never ask you to validate an operation you did not initiate. If you receive such a request, do not answer, lock your card if you shared its details, and contact your adviser or the opposition line. Report phishing emails to phishing@demobank.example.
