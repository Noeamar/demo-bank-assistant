Default limits: Visa Classic allows 1,500 EUR of payments per rolling 30 days and 500 EUR of withdrawals per 7 days. Visa Premier allows 3,000 EUR of payments per 30 days and 1,000 EUR of withdrawals per 7 days. Mastercard Gold allows 4,000 EUR of payments per 30 days and 1,500 EUR of withdrawals per 7 days. You can raise your limits temporarily for 30 days in the app; this requires strong authentication. Permanent changes are made with your adviser.
