Customer service advisers are available by phone Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 20:00 and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. Secure messages sent from the app are answered within 48 working hours. You can book an appointment with your branch adviser in the app (Contact > Book an appointment). The opposition line for lost or stolen cards is open 24/7.
