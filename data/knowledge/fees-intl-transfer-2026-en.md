title: International transfer fees (2026 tariff)
lang: en
topic: fees
valid_from: 2026-01-01
---
SEPA transfers in euros are free, including instant transfers. Transfers outside the SEPA area cost 0.1% of the amount, with a minimum of 15 EUR and a maximum of 60 EUR, plus any fees charged by correspondent banks. Currency conversion uses the Demo Bank daily rate. This tariff applies from 1 January 2026.
