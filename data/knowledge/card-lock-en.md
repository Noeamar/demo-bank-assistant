title: Temporarily lock or unlock your card
lang: en
topic: cards
valid_from: 2025-01-01
---
You can temporarily lock your bank card at any time, for free, in the Demo Bank app (Cards > Temporary lock) or through the assistant. The lock is immediate: card payments and cash withdrawals are refused while the card is locked. It is reversible: unlocking re-enables the card instantly but requires strong authentication (a one-time secure code). A temporary lock is useful if you misplaced your card. If your card is lost or stolen, you must also declare an opposition, which blocks it permanently.
