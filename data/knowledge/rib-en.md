lang: en

valid_from: 2025-01-01

Your bank details (RIB) including your IBAN and BIC can be downloaded as a PDF in the app (Accounts > select an account > Bank details) or in online banking. You can share them by email directly from the app. Never share your card number, secure codes or passwords with anyone who asks for your bank details.
