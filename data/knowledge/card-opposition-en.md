title: Lost or stolen card: declare an opposition
lang: en
topic: cards
valid_from: 2025-01-01
---
If your card is lost or stolen, lock it immediately, then declare an opposition. The opposition permanently blocks the card. You can declare it 24/7 in the app (Cards > Declare loss or theft) or by calling the opposition line on +33 1 00 00 00 00 (demo number). In case of theft, file a police report. A replacement card is sent within 5 business days; one replacement per year is free of charge. An adviser can help you review recent transactions and dispute fraudulent payments.
