lang: en
topic: contact
valid_from: 2025-01-01

You can file a complaint with your adviser, by secure message, or through the complaints form in the app. Demo Bank acknowledges every complaint within 10 business days and sends a response within 2 months at the latest. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can contact the Demo Bank mediator free of charge.
